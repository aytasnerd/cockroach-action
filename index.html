A protest ends when everyone goes home. A demand list does not. It says exactly what is being asked for, shows how many people stand behind each one, and puts the phone numbers and emails of every sitting MP one tap away. No leader to arrest, no account to ban. It lives on every phone that opens it.

How it works

  1. Vote on one shared list

    Everyone opens the same demands in the same order. The number beside each one is the real total across everyone who backed it, not a tally stuck on your own phone.

  2. Send it in one tap

    Search your MP by name or state, or pick the testing agency or the ministry. A message is written for you. You edit it, then send it from your own WhatsApp or email. Nothing ever goes out on its own.

  3. Make it official

    Turn any demand into an RTI application under the Right to Information Act, 2005. Once you file it, a reply is legally due within thirty days.

Anyone can add a demand

Write one and it goes into a queue. A few people from the chapter read what comes in and publish what belongs, so no stranger can quietly rewrite what the group is asking for. Every decision is logged and open to see.

Why call it Cockroach Action

Because a cockroach outlasts whatever is thrown at it. Cut the power, block a site, arrest an organizer, and the list is still sitting on every phone that opened it, ready to come back the moment one of them finds signal.
