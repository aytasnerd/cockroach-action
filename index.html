How it works

  1. Vote on the list

    Everyone sees the same demands in the same order. The count is the real total across everyone who voted, not a number kept on your own phone.

  2. Send it

    Pick a demand and a contact. The message is written for you and opens in WhatsApp, your mail app, or X. You read it and press send yourself.

  3. Make it official

    Turn any demand into an RTI application under the Right to Information Act, 2005. A reply is legally due in 30 days.

Why a cockroach

Because they outlast the people trying to get rid of them. There is no account to ban and no office to shut. The list lives on every phone that has opened it, and it catches up the moment anyone is online.

Anyone can add a demand

Write one and it goes into a queue. A few people from your chapter read what comes in and publish what belongs on the list, so a stranger cannot quietly rewrite what your group is asking for. Every decision is recorded.
